    /* Blood (TraditionalPreservationFishMeatBlood.lua; user, 2026-11-13). Blood from vanilla's bleeding goes off
       through TP Core's FluidAgeing; salt or vinegar keeps it longer. One mix makes either shape: 1 L of blood
       (fresh or cured, never spoiled), a filler: a quarter of a bag of oats or rice (12 uses; rice as for
       morcilla) or, renewable, 12 grains of barley, rye or wheat (0.24 kg). The filler line keeps the one it takes,
       and TP Core's Charge takes the real amount (F.FILLER); the craft window shows it. half a block of
       Lard or Tallow or a piece of raw fat, and a use of salt. Its food is the sum of what goes in: about 3,200 kcal
       in 1.5 kg (real black pudding runs 250-380 kcal a 100 g). Oats, fat and salt are taken by uses (hunger
       points), so 12 of a 50-hunger bag of oats is a quarter. The casings and pans keep vanilla's icons; the pudding
       and the sausages have their own (tools/build_meat_icons.py), the cooked link nearly black (Item_BloodSausage_Cooked,
       which the game takes for a cooked one). */

    /* The finished foods are made cooked (TP Core's OnCreateCooked), so they read Cooked and can be warmed or fried again. */
